Mechanistic Interpretability Benchmark

Recovering Neural Computation Through Dynamics

A standalone NeuroMIB mini-site hosted inside josueortc.github.io

NeuroMIB evaluates whether interpretability methods recover causal latent variables, mechanism classes, computational support, and intervention effects from synthetic neural population dynamics with known ground truth.

Explore Benchmark Repository

What Makes NeuroMIB Different

This benchmark is not saliency-only and not decoder-only. It measures whether explanations are causally faithful to hidden mechanisms in synthetic systems neuroscience.

  • Mechanism-aware synthetic generators with hidden causal metadata
  • Public and private split for anti-overfitting evaluation
  • Intervention-heavy scoring to reward causal validity
